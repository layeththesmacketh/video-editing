# Lattimore Taste

Read this before any creative pass. This is the editorial brain.

## Who is the work for

Nick Lattimore directs and edits branded campaigns, fashion films, celebrity interviews, and behind-the-scenes for luxury and lifestyle brands. Recent and recurring clients: Vera Wang, Mugler, Calvin Klein, Laneige, Maybelline, Oribe, Effy, Mejuri, Thorne, Reebok, Alo, IM8 Health. Heavy NYFW coverage. Long-form interviews for GQ, Marie Claire, and similar outlets.

## Sensibility, in one breath

Editorial-not-commercial. Talent-led narratives. Behind-the-scenes intimacy. Reactions over action. Kinetic, but breathing.

## What that means in the cut

**Editorial-not-commercial.** A spot can be branded and still feel like a magazine — the brand is a frame, not the subject. If a moment reads as "ad," reach for the rawer alt take.

**Talent-led.** The subject's voice runs the piece. We sequence around what they actually said and how they said it, not around what we wish they had said. Their pauses, half-laughs, restarts — those are texture, not mistakes.

**Reactions = HERO.** A reaction shot is more important than the action that triggered it. Cut to faces. Hold faces. The reaction is where meaning lives.

**Raw + premium.** Handheld, room tone, available light — but graded clean and finished tight. The seams show on purpose; the craft is invisible.

**Kinetic but breathing.** Cuts move, but they don't chatter. Opens carry weight: the first 3–7 seconds must earn the next 30. Then we accelerate.

**Conversational, not punchy.** This is not a YouTube hook-stack. Cadence is human. We let a thought finish.

**Music sits under, never on top.** Score under; never let music carry emotion the cut hasn't earned. If you need a swell to make a moment land, the moment isn't there yet.

## Pacing rules of thumb

- Opening 3–7s: one image or one line, holding. No quick-cut barrage.
- A-roll **spine** + B-roll **relief**. Lay-overs are not wallpaper; each one earns a reason.
- Lay-overs **arrive 1–3 seconds after a line lands**. Not on the cut. After the land.
- **Dwell** when the talent says something true. Don't cover it.
- Reaction shots get held a beat longer than feels comfortable.

## Vocabulary

- **Spine** — A-roll backbone (interview).
- **Lay-over** — B-roll riding over the spine on V2.
- **Bed** — music under everything on A2.
- **Punctuation** — a short cut (≤ 1s) that lands a beat.
- **Dwell** — holding a shot past comfort on purpose.
- **Push-in / pull-out** — slow dolly or scale move; earned, not default.
- **Land** — the moment a line resolves.
- **Ride** — let a shot or line breathe.
- **Earn** — every cut, push, music swell paid for by what came before.

## Anti-patterns

- Punching in on every line.
- Music swells on a line that didn't earn one.
- B-roll over a reaction.
- Symmetric cutting (line, B-roll, line, B-roll, line, B-roll). Vary it.
- "Commercial" closes — sting + logo + button. We resolve, then leave.

## Pre-flight checklist (before exporting)

- [ ] First 3–7s carry weight without a stinger.
- [ ] No B-roll over a hook line or a reaction.
- [ ] ≤ 60% of runtime under B-roll.
- [ ] ≥ 0.5s of bare A-roll between adjacent lay-overs.
- [ ] Music sits under; no swell carrying an unearned beat.
- [ ] Arc is legible: hook → premise → texture → turn → resolve.
